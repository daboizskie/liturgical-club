St. Tarcisius

Patron Saint of Altar Servers

Saint Tarcisius was a young Christian martyr who died in the 3rd century defending the Eucharist. He is venerated as the patron saint of altar servers and First Communicants — a model of courage and devotion in service to the Lord.
